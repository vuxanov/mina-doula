Doula vs. midwife: what is the difference?

Written by Mina, doula and birth photographer

Published 17 September 2026

Last reviewed 24 September 2026

A doula offers continuous, non-medical emotional and practical support through pregnancy, birth, and postpartum. A doula does not replace a qualified maternity care professional.

A midwife is a regulated health professional who provides care during pregnancy, labour, and the postnatal period. Their scope includes monitoring, recognising complications, and accessing medical help when needed; the exact tasks and prescribing authority depend on local law and professional scope.

This article is general information, not medical advice. Discuss your individual care plan with a qualified maternity professional.
