What to expect from a home birth in Portugal as a foreigner

Written by Mina, doula and birth photographer

Published 17 September 2026

Last reviewed 24 September 2026

If you are planning a home birth, arrange care early with a qualified maternal and obstetric health professional. This gives you time to discuss whether home birth is suitable for you, make a transfer plan, and understand any fees or insurance cover.

For birth registration, you will need a document proving the birth. Check the current process with the Portuguese Civil Registry and tell them if the birth took place at home.

This article is general information, not medical or legal advice. For decisions about your care, speak directly with your maternity professional and confirm current requirements with the relevant Portuguese authority.
